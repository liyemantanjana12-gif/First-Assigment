What Filmmaking Means To Me

To me, filmmaking is a reflection of the world, both external and internal. It allows us to explore realities, fears, beliefs, dreams and experiences. Through film, something imagined or make-believe can feel real, while something that is real can be presented in a way that makes us see it differently. Filmmaking allows me to see myself and others reflected on screen, while also creating worlds and stories that may never exist outside of our imagination. It is a way of exploring what we know, what we believe and what we fear, and turning those ideas into something that other people can see, feel and connect with.
